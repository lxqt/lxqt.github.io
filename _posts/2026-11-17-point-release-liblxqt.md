The LXQt team announces a point release of **liblxqt 2.5.1**

Changes:

 * Open backlight helper stream with close-on-exec (to prevent a freeze in apps like lxqt-panel).
 * Silenced a clang warning.

<br/>
The release can be downloaded from [Github](https://github.com/lxqt/liblxqt/releases).
