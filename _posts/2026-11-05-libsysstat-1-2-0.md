The LXQt team announces the release of libsysstat 1.2.0.
The release can be downloaded from [Github](https://github.com/lxqt/libsysstat/releases).

Changes:

 * Used the operators of `Qt::Literals::StringLiterals` instead of `QString::fromLatin1()`, `QLatin1String()` and `QLatin1Char()`.


<br/>
A full list of changes is in the CHANGELOG file.
